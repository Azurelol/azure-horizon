### Experience Triggers

- You fix something broken for someone in need.
- You create a new device.
- You discover new technology.
- You fight a giant mechanical monstrosity.

---

### Complications

- You break something you shouldn't have.
- One of your creations goes haywire.
- One of your precious gadgets breaks.
- Someone makes use of one of your inventions with an evil intent.
