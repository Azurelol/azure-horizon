### Experience Triggers

- You uncover forgotten knowledge.
- You convince someone through logical arguments.
- You share your knowledge with someone influential.
- You successfully recall a rule your director doesn't remember.

---

### Complications

- You learn something you really shouldn't have.
- A secret society intends to silence you.
- You bore someone.
