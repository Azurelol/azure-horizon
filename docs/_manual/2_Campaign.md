# Preface

<p>This chapter covers both the procedure for starting your campaign in the system and for running your sessions.</p><p>You will learn how to create your setting, give your party a shared goal and start play with a prologue.</p>

# Session Zero



# Episode

<p>To carry on the general framing of our campaign structure the system refers to a session as an <em>episode</em>, with a defined beginning, middle and end, with procedures to go along with it all.</p><p>The intent is to provide directors with a structure they can follow from session to session and players with anticipation for some of the procedures these contain.</p>

# Opening

<p>After both you and your players are ready to begin play, we go on with the <em>opening</em> procedure. It not only includes some things you are familiar with (such as recounting the events of the last session) but a few more, all in a more formalized way.</p>

# Credits
