# Pressure

<p>Adversaries of elite rank and above can be pressured and eventually staggered.</p><p>When an adversary is staggered, they take an increased amount of damage from all sources and  are considered stunned.</p>

# Formations

<p>During a combat scene, each character or adversary is positioned in either the front row or the back row. </p><ul><li><p>For each character in the front row, a column is defined numerically starting from 1 at the very top.</p></li><li><p>There must be at least 1 more character in the front row than the back row at all times.</p></li><li><p>Characters in the back row cannot attack with melee weapons.</p></li><li><p>Characters in the back row must have an assigned column at all times. </p></li><li><p>Characters in the front row that have a character behind them in the back row are considered to be giving them cover.</p></li><li><p>At the start of combat, all characters can freely assign themselves places in their formations.</p></li><li><p>During combat, characters can voluntarily change their place in the formation using the Swap maneuver. </p></li><li><p>During combat, certain actions can shift characters around their formation.</p></li><li><p>During combat, when a character in the front row is defeated while providing cover, the character in the back row is shifted to the front row.</p></li><li><p>When a character is pushed or pulled, it trades places with another character in that column.</p></li><li><p>Particularly large creatures take up both rows.</p></li></ul>

# Preface

<p>This chapter will cover the rules for running battles in the system. A battle is the scene that takes place when the party encounters adversaries who wish to do them harm and they must defend themselves.</p>

# Initiative

<p>Both heroes and adversaries roll initiative every round, then alternate taking turns in the initiative order beginning with the side that has priority. This is usually the heroes unless a villain is present, in which case adversaries go first.</p><blockquote><p>By default the system automatically handles rolling initiative for all combatants at the start of the round for the sake of expediency. In a battle scene every second counts.</p></blockquote>

# Victory

<p>The battle is over once the victory condition has been met. When that happens the director will briefly recount the aftermath before assigning a <em>grade</em> depending on the player's performance in the battle.</p>
