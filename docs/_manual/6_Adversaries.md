<p>We define adversaries as the individuals who oppose the heroes from either an ideological standpoint or due to their nature.</p>

# Intent

<p>An adversary's role and rank determines their intents, which are the actions they will take each round. For example a Brute will prioritize performing strong attacks and occasionally enhancing themselves.</p>
