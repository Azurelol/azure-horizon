<img src="systems/azure-horizon/assets/docs/images/rest.png"><h2>Short Rest</h2><p>During short rest scenes, for every 2 PCs in the party one of them can perform an activity.</p><p>You should rotate among yourselves and avoid consecutive repeats.</p><h2>Long Rest</h2><p>We call the times when the heroes retire for the day at a safe location (such as a settlement or at camp) <em>long rests.</em> In these locations the heroes are able to restock their supplies, unwind and fully prepare for their next day.</p><p>During long rest scenes, each PC <em>fully recovers</em> their hit points, mind points, inventory points and clears all their tension points.</p>
