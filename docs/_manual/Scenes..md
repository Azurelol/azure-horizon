---
title: Scenes
---

<p>An episode consists of many scenes that stitch the narrative together and propel it forward.</p><ul><li><p>Rest: A scene where the party rests for the day.</p></li><li><p>Combat: A scene where the party encounters adversaries in deadly combat.</p></li><li><p>Villain: A scene where the campaign’s adversaries act off-screen.</p></li></ul><p></p>
