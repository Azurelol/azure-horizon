My name is Christian Sagel and I am the developer of this VTTRPG system.

- My favorite video game genre is the JRPG.
- I play TTRPGs once a week as a GM.
- I spend a great deal of time thinking about RPG design.

### Background

<p>I have been playing role-playing games for over 30 years, ranging from western CRPGs to my preferred genre (and the inspiration of this system), JRPGs.</p>
<p>I took an interest in the TTRPG hobby in 2021 throughout the height of COVID, starting with Pathfinder 2E for a few years before branching out, trying out other systems and learning about a great deal about many others.</p>
<p>I have a professional background in game development and software engineering.</p>

## Goals

<p>
I am driven to develop a game I would like to play weekly with my home group. I have tried quite a few systems over the years and the closest that came to matching my preferred game-mastery style was Fabula Ultima.
</p>
<p>
It is a fantastic game in the space and I have both ran it and developed for it over the past couple years. But there are many things I would like to do differently.
</p>
<p>
Thus, I would like to give it a shot myself. It has been certainly overwhelming at times, but I believe I have what it takes to develop a game I would like to run, that my players would enjoy taking part in, and that others could come to enjoy as well.
</p>
