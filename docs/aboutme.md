My name is Christian Sagel and I am the developer of this VTTRPG system.

- My favorite game genre are JRPGs.
- I play TTRPGs once a week as a GM.
- I spend way too much thinking about RPG design.

### Background

I have been playing role-playing games for over 30 years, ranging from western CRPGs to my preferred genre (and the inspiration of this system), JRPGs.
I took an interest in the TTRPG hobby in 2021 throughout the height of COVID and started with Pathfinder 2E for a few years before trying out other systems and learning about a great deal more.

## Goals

I am highly motivated to develop a game I would like to play weekly with my now-established home group. I have tried quite a few over the years and the closest that came to matching my preferred game-mastery style was Fabula Ultima.
It is a fantastic game in the space and I have both ran it and developed for it over the past couple years. But there are many things I would like to do differently than it.
Thus, I would like to give it a shot myself. It has been certainly overwhelming at times, but I believe I have what it takes to develop a game I would like to run, that my players would enjoy taking part in, and that others could come to enjoy as well.
