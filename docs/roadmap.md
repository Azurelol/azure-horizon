This page will cover the currently planned development milestones of the system, both as development and business goals.
It is true, I would like to make a living out of developing this system, so I can keep doing it!

# Playtest

The system will first be play-tested in private before being available to a select group of few.

# System Release

The system will be released on the Foundry Marketplace as a premium system.

# Digital Book Crowdfunding

If the system is a success at this point, the plan would be to set up a campaign to publish a digital rendition of the
rules of the system will as an e-book, commissioning art pieces for it. It would then be published on a digital RPG marketplace.

# Premium Modules

Once the system is out, there are plenty of opportunities for useful content and add-ons that could be developed for it on Foundry.
Besides visual enhancements, I believe the publication of adventure kits would be of a great help to game masters so that those who lack the means or time are able
to bootstrap their campaigns with minimal effort.
